# Video Game Sales Dashboard — Power BI

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing global video game sales across regions, platforms, and genres from 1980 to 2017.

## Business Questions
- Which regions dominate global video game sales?
- Which platforms and genres drive the most volume?
- How has the industry evolved year over year?

## Key Findings
- **8,815M** total copies sold across the analyzed period
- **North America** leads with 49% of global sales
- **PS2** is the top platform by total volume
- Industry peaked in **2008-2009** with 600M+ annual copies sold
- **Action** and **Sports** are the dominant genres globally

## Dashboard Features
- Dynamic slicers by Year, Region, Genre, Platform, and Publisher
- Stacked bar chart — sales trend by year and region (1980–2017)
- Donut chart — regional market share breakdown
- Horizontal bar charts — platform and genre performance
- Detail table — product-level sales drill-down

## Tech Stack
- **Power BI** (Web) — dashboard design and interactivity
- **PowerQuery** — data transformation and standardization
- **Data Source** — Video Game Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
